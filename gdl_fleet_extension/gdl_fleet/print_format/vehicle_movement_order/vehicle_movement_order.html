{#- Vehicle Movement Order - GDL's own printed form, reproduced. Deliberate choices, so nobody "corrects" them later: * The form carries EIGHT items and a signature block. Nothing else. An earlier draft added a logo, a ruled box, ruled value lines, the department beside the office, the internal vehicle id beside the registration and the origin beside the destination. GDL looked at it and said the document's own fields are enough, so all of that is gone. If a field is not on the paper form, it does not belong here. * Items 9 and 10 of the paper form are not printed. GDL asked for both to be dropped. They were the return reading and the utilising officer's signature - filled in after the journey, on a form handed over before it. * Item 7 prints the departure time, and the Departure Odometer when the order carries one. It usually will not: the sheet is printed before the vehicle moves and the reading is taken at the gate, so a blank space is printed instead, as on the paper form. Both cases are normal. * Item 8 is worked out from departure to expected return. The paper form asks for days/hours, so that is what it says. * The signature block is left blank to be signed, as on the original. -#} {#- An order raised without a site still belongs to the office that owns the vehicle, and the form has to name one. -#} {%- set office = doc.site or (frappe.db.get_value("Vehicle", doc.vehicle, "site") if doc.vehicle else None) -%} {%- set driver_name = frappe.db.get_value("Driver", doc.driver, "full_name") if doc.driver else None -%} {%- set hours = frappe.utils.time_diff_in_hours(doc.expected_return, doc.departure_datetime) if (doc.expected_return and doc.departure_datetime) else None -%}
