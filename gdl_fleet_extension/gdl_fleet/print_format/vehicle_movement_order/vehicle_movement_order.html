An earlier draft added a logo, a ruled box, ruled value lines, the department beside the office, the internal vehicle id beside the registration and the origin beside the destination. GDL looked at it and said the document's own fields are enough, so all of that is gone. If a field is not on the paper form, it does not belong here. * Items 9 and 10 of the paper form are not printed. GDL asked for both to be dropped. They were the return reading and the utilising officer's signature - filled in after the journey, on a form handed over before it. * Item 7 prints the departure time, and the Departure Odometer when the order carries one. It usually will not: the sheet is printed before the vehicle moves and the reading is taken at the gate, so a blank space is printed instead, as on the paper form. Both cases are normal. * Item 8 is worked out from departure to expected return.
